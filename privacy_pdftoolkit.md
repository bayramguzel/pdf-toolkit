# Privacy Policy for PDF Toolkit

**Effective Date:** November 14, 2025  
**Last Updated:** November 14, 2025

## Introduction

PDF Toolkit ("we," "our," or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how PDF Toolkit handles your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Data Processed Locally

PDF Toolkit processes all PDF files and documents **locally on your device**. We do not upload, store, or transmit any of your PDF files, documents, images, or personal data to our servers or any third-party servers.

The app may access:

- PDF files you select for processing
- Device storage through the system picker or app-controlled folders to save processed files
- Images you choose to convert into PDFs through the system file picker

### Data Transferred Outside Your Device

Some actions can move data outside your device because you explicitly choose them:

- Sharing a PDF sends the selected file to the app or service you choose in the share sheet
- Sending feedback opens your email app so your message is handled by your chosen mail provider

## How We Use Your Information

- **File Processing:** All PDF operations (merge, split, compress, encrypt, etc.) happen entirely on your device
- **Settings Storage:** Your app preferences (theme, save location) are stored locally using SharedPreferences

## Data Sharing

We **do not sell, rent, or share** your personal information or PDF content with third parties, except:

- **User-initiated sharing:** When you choose to share a file through the operating system's share sheet
- **Legal Requirements:** If required by law or to protect our rights

## Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

- **Delete Data:** Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data
- **Control Permissions:** Manage storage and file access permissions in device settings
- **Contact Us:** Request information about your data

## Data Security

- All file processing happens **offline** on your device
- No PDF content is transmitted over the internet
- App settings are stored locally on your device
- We implement standard security measures appropriate for local processing

## Children's Privacy

PDF Toolkit does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with information, please contact us.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on our website and within the app. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
